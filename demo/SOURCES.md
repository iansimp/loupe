# Demo photos — sources

These are real photographs, not generated. They are here only so the Loupe clickable demo can show what a listing photo and a stolen marketplace page look like. None of these makers are Loupe customers. Nothing has been filed.

News photos are still copyrighted by the outlets named below. Product photos belong to the makers. Do not reuse this folder outside the local demo.
